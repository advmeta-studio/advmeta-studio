Frequently Asked Questions

Before you order.

What happens after I pay?

Send your business name, slogan, preferred colors, industry, reference images, and any design ideas through the contact method you already use with adVmeta Studio. Your package and payment will identify the level of work included.

Does 24-hour delivery mean the entire project is finished?

The 24-hour promise refers to your first concepts or first draft. Final completion depends on your revision requests and response time.

Are the designs original?

Yes. Concepts are built around your project direction. Inspiration images help communicate style, but your delivered design should be customized to your brand.

What file types will I receive?

File types depend on the package. Copper includes PNG and JPG. Higher tiers add transparent, PDF, social-media, and vector/source-file options as listed.

Can I use the logo commercially?

Yes. Commercial-use rights are included as stated in each package. Save your project files and payment receipt for your business records.

Are payments secure?

Yes. Package payments are processed through Stripe's hosted checkout pages. adVmeta Studio does not directly collect or store your card information on this website.
